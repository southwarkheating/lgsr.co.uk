Questions

Is there a call-out charge?

No. The prices below are the full price of the visit.

What counts as an extra appliance?

The £95 certificate covers the boiler and the gas hob or cooker. Anything else, such as a gas fire, a second boiler or a gas water heater, is £25 each.

Do prices include VAT?

The prices shown are what you pay.

What if a repair is needed?

We tell you the fault and give you a fixed price before doing any work. You decide.
