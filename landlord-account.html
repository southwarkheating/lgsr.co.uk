Landlord account

Every certificate, due date and invoice for your properties, in one place.

All your gas safety records. One login.

Your LGSR landlord account runs on the Southwark Heating customer system, so every visit, certificate and invoice we do for you is there automatically.

Sign in to your accountGet an account with a contract

What you can do in your account

  • See every property and its gas safety certificate
  • Download certificates and job photos to send to tenants
  • See which certificates are due next
  • View and pay invoices securely
  • Check upcoming bookings
  • Update your own contact details

How to get an account

Your account is set up when you book your first visit with us. You sign in with the email address you booked with. Letting agents can ask for an agent account covering all their landlords.

You will sign in on southwarkheating.com, because Southwark Heating’s Gas Safe engineers carry out every LGSR visit. It is the same account.
