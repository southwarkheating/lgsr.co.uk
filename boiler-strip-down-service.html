Boiler strip-down service

The deep clean your boiler needs every few years. £175, or £260 with your gas safety certificate.

Burner out. Heat exchanger clean. Boiler ready for winter.

A standard boiler service checks the boiler is working safely and efficiently. A strip-down service goes further: the engineer removes the burner and fan assembly to clean the inside of the heat exchanger and the parts that cause most breakdowns.

Signs your boiler needs a strip-down

  • Poor flue gas readings on the last service
  • Ignition faults or the boiler locking out
  • Kettling, roaring or whistling noises
  • Condensate trap or sump blockages
  • No strip-down in the last 2 to 3 years

Questions

How long does a strip-down service take?

Usually 1.5 to 3 hours, depending on the boiler and how dirty it is.

How often should I have a strip-down service?

Many engineers recommend one every 2 to 3 years, or sooner if the boiler is older, lives in a hard water area or has had combustion problems. London is a hard water area.

What if a part needs replacing?

Seals, gaskets and electrodes sometimes need renewing once disturbed. We tell you the price before fitting anything.
