Free renewal reminders

We remind you before your landlord gas safety certificate runs out. Free, for any property.

Never miss a gas safety check again.

Missing a gas safety check puts your tenants at risk and leaves you open to prosecution. Tell us when your certificate runs out and we will remind you in good time. It is free, whoever did your last check.

How reminders work

  • We remind you about 2 months before the expiry date, then again closer to the date
  • Book inside the 2-month window and your new certificate runs from the old expiry date, so you lose nothing
  • One tap to book, with your property details already filled in
  • Add as many properties as you like
