How contracts work

  1. Tell us your properties and their current expiry dates.
  2. We add them to your landlord account.
  3. Before each due date we contact you and your tenant and book the check.
  4. The certificate is emailed to you and saved in your account.

Questions

Is there a minimum term?

No. Contracts roll on each year and you can cancel at any time. You only pay for visits we carry out.

Do all properties have to be renewed on the same date?

No. Each property keeps its own date. We book each one inside its 2-month window.

Can I add properties later?

Yes. Add or remove properties at any time, and your rate moves up or down with your portfolio size.
