Ideal Logic strip-down service

What our engineers do on a Ideal Logic strip-down, step by step.

Ideal Logic boilers have a fan and burner assembly on the front of the heat exchanger, with a condensate trap and sump below. This is what our Gas Safe engineers do on a Ideal Logic strip-down service. It covers Logic+ and Logic Max combi, system and heat boilers.

Step by step

  1. Isolate and open up. Turn off the gas, power and water, remove the case and note any fault codes.
  2. Remove fan and burner. Disconnect the gas and electrics and remove the fan and burner assembly.
  3. Inspect burner and gaskets. Check the burner and the burner gasket, renewing any gasket that is damaged.
  4. Clean the heat exchanger. Clean the combustion side of the heat exchanger and clear the sump.
  5. Clean the condensate trap. Remove and clean the trap. Blocked condensate is one of the most common Ideal Logic faults.
  6. Check the electrode. Inspect the ignition electrode, check the gap and renew it if worn.
  7. Rebuild and test. Reassemble, gas tightness test, and flue gas analysis to Ideal’s limits.
  8. Final checks. Check the expansion vessel, system pressure, controls and safety devices.
Common Ideal Logic faults a strip-down helps prevent: L2 ignition lockouts, F2 flame loss and condensate blockages.

Price

A Ideal Logic strip-down service is £175, or £260 with a landlord gas safety certificate on the same visit. If a seal, gasket or electrode needs renewing, we tell you the price before fitting it.
