How to read your gas safety record

  • Flue type: RS is room sealed (the safest type, like most modern boilers). OF is open flued, which takes air from the room, so ventilation and spillage tests matter. FL is flueless, like a gas hob.
  • Pressure or heat input: proves the appliance burns the right amount of gas. Here, the boiler was measured at 25.12 kW.
  • Flue flow and spillage: show fumes leave the building and do not come back into the room. Needed on open flued appliances like this gas fire.
  • Safe to use: the engineer’s verdict for each appliance. All three passed.
  • Next safety check due: the date your next landlord gas safety check must be done by.
  • CO detector tested: in a rented home in England, a carbon monoxide alarm is required in any room with a fixed combustion appliance, such as a gas fire or boiler, but not a gas cooker. We can supply and fit one.
