Landlord gas safety certificate (CP12)

The annual gas safety check every landlord needs, for a fixed £95. Gas Safe engineers, certificate emailed after the visit.

Your gas safety certificate, done right and on time.

A landlord gas safety certificate, also called a CP12, LGSR or landlord gas safety record, is the document a Gas Safe registered engineer issues after a gas safety check of a rented property. It shows each gas appliance and flue has been tested and is safe to use.

If you rent out a home with gas, the law says you must have a gas safety check every 12 months and give your tenants a copy of the record. We do landlord gas safety certificates across South London, south-east London, Bromley, Bexley and north-west Kent for a fixed £95.

What’s included in a £95 gas safety certificate

  • A full gas safety check of the boiler and the gas hob or cooker
  • Flue flow and spillage tests, and a check of the flue route and termination
  • Ventilation checks for each appliance
  • Operating pressure or heat input recorded
  • Safety devices checked for correct operation
  • Gas pipework tightness test, and emergency control valve checks
  • Smoke alarms and CO alarms tested where fitted
  • Your landlord gas safety record emailed to you after the visit

Each extra gas appliance, such as a gas fire or a second boiler, is £25.

Gas safety certificate or boiler service?

They are different jobs. A gas safety certificate checks that appliances are safe. A boiler service cleans, adjusts and tests the boiler so it keeps working and stays in warranty. A boiler can pass a gas safety check and still break down next week if it has not been serviced.

Who needs a landlord gas safety record?

  • Private landlords letting a house or flat with any gas appliance
  • HMO landlords, including rooms let individually (HMO gas safety)
  • Holiday let and short let owners
  • Letting agents acting for landlords (agent accounts)
  • Owners selling or buying, who want an independent homeowner gas safety check

Questions

What is a CP12?

CP12 was the name of the old CORGI form for a landlord gas safety record. The name stuck, so most people still call the certificate a CP12.

How long does a gas safety certificate last?

12 months from the date of the check. Book the next one up to 2 months early and the new certificate runs from the old expiry date.

How long does the check take?

Usually 30 to 60 minutes for a typical flat or house with a boiler and hob. Add a boiler service and allow 1 to 2 hours.

What if an appliance fails?

We explain the fault, make it safe and give you a fixed price for the repair. See what happens if a gas safety check fails.

Do I need to be there?

No. Most landlords are not. We arrange access with your tenant or agent, or collect keys by arrangement.
